## BOX 8: THE SHORT VERSION

- status: dropped
- section: 6.9 The Limits of Power: Volunteers and the Implementer Veto

**THE SHORT VERSION**

Two rooms design (EWG, LEWG), two rooms word (CWG, LWG), fifteen study groups feed them, and the plenary adopts. C++ ships every three years, a model in place since 2012. SC22 appoints the convener; the convener appoints the chairs; the Direction Group sets priorities and chairs schedule by them. The rules are in eight standing documents, SD-4 above all. Nobody can be ordered; the implementers can decline to build, but that is not a veto, and the committee has too often gone ahead without them.
