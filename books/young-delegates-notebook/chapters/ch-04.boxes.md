## BOX 5: THE SHORT VERSION

- status: dropped
- section: 5.8 Three Homes for a Library: Boost, Beman, and the Standard

**THE SHORT VERSION**

You can read, review and discuss from home. After attending a committee meeting, you can request access to the reflectors. You may share straw poll questions and numerical results after they are published on the public Github tracker, but get someone’s consent before publicly quoting their words or attributing a position to them. Be polite and respectful in committee email and chat. isocpp.org is official, open-std.org is the archive, wg21.org is the Alliance's unofficial search view. The wiki and Mattermost open once you're in. Float ideas on std-proposals, sit in on telecons (about thirty a month), and read the trip reports. Boost reviews and lets libraries grow, Beman proves a paper's library before the vote, the standard freezes it forever: ick the home by how much change your users can afford.
