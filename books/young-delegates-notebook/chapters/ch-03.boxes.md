## BOX 3: WATCH OUT

- status: dropped
- section: 4.2 What a Paper Is, and the Numbers It Carries

**WATCH OUT**

"N-number" does not mean "administrative, skip it." The one N-document you most need, the working draft, is an N-paper. The publishing date is also important. Before 2015, N-papers functioned as proposals.

P-papers don’t get a new number when there is a new revision. P1234R0 and P1234R1 are different revisions of the same paper. But N-papers get a new number with each revision. N5008 and N5054 are the same paper with different revisions.

## BOX 6: THE SHORT VERSION

- status: dropped
- section: 4.7 The Documents Everyone Should Read: SD-4 and the Direction Papers

**THE SHORT VERSION**

No paper, no proposal. P is a proposal, N is an ISO document (including the working draft), D is a pre-mailing draft, and R counts revisions. wg21.link resolves any number. Mailings are roughly monthly, and the pre-meeting deadline decides what's actionable. The lifecycle runs idea, study group, design, wording, plenary, CD comments, DIS. Read SD-4 and the three Direction Group papers first (all three grew out of the old P0939): P2000 for the medium and long term, P5000 for this cycle's short-term priorities, and P4024 for what to do about a competitor; all three grew out of the old P0939.
