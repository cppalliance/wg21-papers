**RULE OF THUMB**

Give your name and speak into the microphone. Keep to one point and aim to finish within two minutes.

## BOX 3: FROM THE RULEBOOK

- status: dropped
- section: 8.3 Confidentiality and the Code of Conduct

**FROM THE RULEBOOK**

"We follow the ISO Code of Ethics and Conduct (March 2023) and IEC Code of Conduct – together, the Code." Among its lines: "Ensure that all views are heard & understood", "Accept group decisions", and "Avoid metaphors, irony & be aware that jokes and humor may not translate." ([SD-4](https://isocpp.org/std/standing-documents/sd-4-wg21-practices-and-procedures), Code of Conduct)

## BOX 4: RULE OF THUMB

- status: dropped
- section: 8.4 Minute Taking

**RULE OF THUMB**

Scribe early and often. You will understand the issues better than the listeners, you may interrupt the queue for clarification, and the room will remember who kept the record.

## BOX 6: THE SHORT VERSION

- status: dropped
- section: 8.6 Get Out of Your Lane

**THE SHORT VERSION**

Read the papers before joining a session. When speaking, give your name, use the microphone and keep your remarks brief. Follow the confidentiality and conduct guidance in § 8.3. Volunteer to take minutes and ask for clarification when needed. Get to know people during breaks and meals, and try a subgroup outside your specialty.
