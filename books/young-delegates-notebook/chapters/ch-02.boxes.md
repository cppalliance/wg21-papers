## BOX 6: THE SHORT VERSION

- status: dropped
- section: 2.8 Where Ideas Come From, and Where They Get Aired

**THE SHORT VERSION**

Nobody grants permission; you start. WG21 sits inside ISO/IEC JTC1/SC22. Inside WG21 people vote, not countries; countries vote later, on the ballot. A guest comes free on a week's notice and does everything but the plenary poll, but is limited to two meetings. Attendance beyond that needs a National Body and a Global Directory entry: You can come via your own NB (Chapter 3), INCITS takes anyone for a fee, or a free seat sponsored by the C++ Alliance or the Beman Project gets you in without a vote. Then show up, and keep showing up.
