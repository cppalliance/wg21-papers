## BOX 7: THE SHORT VERSION

- status: dropped
- section: 12.7 The Other 80%

**THE SHORT VERSION**

Float on std-proposals first. Use mpark/wg21 or Bikeshed and the isocpp.org template; SD-7 covers numbers, format and deadlines; the isocpp.org papers form assigns the number, increments the R, and has two buttons: Save Draft as often as you like, Save and Mark for Review once. Make it readable on a phone. Put the code on Compiler Explorer and GitHub. Name a presenter, expect five to ten revisions or far more, keep attending, disclose patents. Get on the agenda through whichever channel your chair keeps it in (the tracker, the wiki, the reflector), and email the chair directly if your paper is not there. Design approval is a fifth of the work.
